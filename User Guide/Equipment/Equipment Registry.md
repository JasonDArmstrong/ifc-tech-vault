---

## How to Use This Document

One row per physical device. Fill in every field you can. Leave unknown fields blank rather than guessing. **Serial number and purchase date are the most important fields** — they are needed for warranty claims, insurance, and end-of-life planning. The manual link should point to either the manufacturer's PDF or the component guide in this User Guide.

**Status values:**
- **Active** — in regular use
- **Backup** — works, kept as a spare
- **Needs Service** — in use but has a known issue
- **Retired** — no longer in use, kept on-site
- **Disposed** — removed from inventory
