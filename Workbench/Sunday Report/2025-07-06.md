### What’s different—and what still stands out

| Area                       | What I’m seeing in the new grab                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Good / Needs work                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bandwidth**              | Y-axis now goes to **20 kHz**, so Audacity is rolling at 48 kHz (or 44.1 kHz) instead of the 16 kHz we saw earlier.                                                                                                                   | ✅ Full-range capture secured—nice.                                                                                                                  |
| **Peaks / head-room**      | Wave-tops sit ~6 dB below 0 dBFS. The red record meter no longer hugs the very top.                                                                                                                                                   | ✅ Safe head-room; one loud cough won’t clip.                                                                                                        |
| **Average level**          | Most speech sits around –24 dBFS to –18 dBFS.                                                                                                                                                                                         | ✅ Sweet spot for raw dialogue.                                                                                                                      |
| **Low-end energy**         | The red/orange “carpet” below ≈150 Hz is still strong.                                                                                                                                                                                | ⚠️ Plan on a **high-pass (80–100 Hz, 12 dB/oct)** in post or on the DM3 to tame room rumble & mic-handling noise.                                   |
| **Gating / drop-outs**     | I still see abrupt full-band cut-offs between words (the solid black gaps that come a fraction of a second after each phrase). They’re cleaner than before but suggest **a gate/expander or the wireless mic’s compander** is active. | ⚠️ If you can, **raise the gate’s threshold** (so it only mutes real silence) or disable it—otherwise quiet word endings will keep getting chopped. |
| **High-frequency detail**  | Very little above 8 kHz—which is normal for lapel speech, but the slope looks abrupt.                                                                                                                                                 | 🛈 Nothing “wrong,” just note that later a gentle shelf at 6–8 kHz may restore some brightness if it sounds dull.                                   |
| **Plosive bursts**         | Spikes of red 0–400 Hz at sentence starts show up.                                                                                                                                                                                    | 🛈 Typical for P/B plosives. If they pop on loudspeakers, consider a foam windscreen or 80 Hz high-pass.                                            |
| **Unrendered white block** | The trailing white panel is simply audio that hasn’t finished writing spectrogram tiles while recording.                                                                                                                              | ✅ Normal; it’ll fill in when you stop.                                                                                                              |
