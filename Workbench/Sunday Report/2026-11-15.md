# AM Service

#### Startup
- [ ] Power on cabinet, DM3, cameras, computer — in that order
- [ ] Launch Audacity, then OBS, then Firefox (Open All stream bookmarks)
- [ ] Wireless check — body pack paired, battery above 50%, solid RF signal
- [ ] Camera check — framing and color correct on both, OBS receiving both feeds
- [ ] DM3 — correct preset loaded, gain confirmed, sends active (Computer, Narthex, Main)
- [ ] Stream setup — sermon title and description pasted into Facebook and YouTube, persistent stream key confirmed
- [ ] OBS audio check — meter reading -20 to -15 dB at speaking volume
- [ ] Audacity audio check — meter reading -24 to -15 dB at speaking volume

#### Go Live
- [ ] Congregation mic on (Ch 1) when music starts, narthex volume set
- [ ] Audacity recording started, waveform confirmed moving
- [ ] OBS streaming started — pre-roll to program, watching for dropped frames
- [ ] Facebook and YouTube live started via SermonAudio multicast
- [ ] G1 key pressed, pastor mic on, congregation mic off when pastor reaches pulpit

#### During Service
- [ ] DM3 levels healthy throughout — fader adjusted if needed, no feedback
- [ ] OBS meters, dropped frames, and disk space checked periodically
- [ ] Stream health green on SermonAudio, Facebook, and YouTube

#### Shutdown
- [ ] Audacity stopped and saved at end of sermon — filename: `MM-DD-YYAM`
- [ ] OBS faded to black and stream stopped at end of service
- [ ] All mics off, cameras off, cabinet off, DM3 off, computer restarted

#### Post-Service
- [ ] Audio exported from Audacity (amplify, compress, normalize) and uploaded via FileZilla
- [ ] Video editing scheduled or completed (DaVinci Resolve → YouTube / Facebook)

#### Sermon Notes

(4) Misery, Deliverance, Gratitude
Ephesians 2:1-10

> [Note!] And you were dead in the trespasses and sins 
> 
> Eph 2:2  in which you once walked, following the course of this world, following the prince of the power of the air, the spirit that is now at work in the sons of disobedience— 
> Eph 2:3  among whom we all once lived in the passions of our flesh, carrying out the desires of the body and the mind, and were by nature children of wrath, like the rest of mankind. 
Eph 2:4  But God, being rich in mercy, because of the great love with which he loved us, 
Eph 2:5  even when we were dead in our trespasses, made us alive together with Christ—by grace you have been saved— 
Eph 2:6  and raised us up with him and seated us with him in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus, 
Eph 2:7  so that in the coming ages he might show the immeasurable riches of his grace in kindness toward us in Christ Jesus. 
Eph 2:8  For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God, 
Eph 2:9  not a result of works, so that no one may boast. 
Eph 2:10  For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand, that we should walk in them. 

1. Universal misery of the human race
	1. Paul is negative, 
	2. Sinners are not spiritually dead not ill, in need of new life. 
	3. To Jesus she was only sleeping. 
	4. Can not be earned only given and received
	5. What is our response, unreserved Gratitude
2. What does unconditional friendship look like.
3. We can not boast 
4. God created humans for good works, original purpose, 
5. before we followed the world not we follow the course of eternal life
6. Trust in Christ, repent, and bare fruits

You are a sinner, repent and receive deliverance, and live a fruitful life.
