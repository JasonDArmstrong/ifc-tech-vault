#### Sermon Notes
(15) The Person of Jesus

Psalm of the week 144.  God is a personal God. David says that God is MY shield, not a shield. He is a shield to each and every one of us individually. David paints a picture of the terrible wrath of God, but we should not fear because God is our God.

Scripture lesson: Galatians 4:4-7.  Do you remember meeting someone who is not very close to you? They were a total stranger, and your knowledge of them grew exponentially. Over time through deliberate action, your relation deepened through increased knowledge. You learned facts about that person as well as un-quantifiable knowledge. This is how our relationship with Jesus is. Read Jesus' ministry not with the factual knowledge that you learn about other historical figures, but read about him like you're becoming closer with a friend. 3 truths about his person:

1. Jesus is eternal God. Jesus was not created when he was born, nor was he created long before then. He was eternally begotten. Jesus is always begotten because of his relationship with the Father, so that his begotten-ness is a passive trait, not an event. His begotten-ness distinguishes him from the angel. Like Melchizedek, he has no genealogy, no beginning, and no end. Not only is he eternal, Jesus is also co-equal and consubstantial with the Father, as touching his divinity. Jesus is God. 1 John 5:20, "He is the true God."  He was asked by one of his disciples can you show us the Father. He responded, "Whoever has seen me has seen the Father." Jesus' name, Immanuel, means God with us.
2. God's son became true man. The apostle John makes this crucial. Great is the mystery of godliness. People said in the early church that true God and true man was impossible and tried to come up with new solutions for how this could be possible. they were all wrong. Is anything too great for God (this morning's service)? Some said he only appeared to be, some say he had an angelic body, but he was born of a woman in flesh and blood. He grew tired. And hungry. And thirsty. And irritated and sad and seldom joyous and hurt. After 40 days in the forest he was hungry. VERY. He was true flesh and blood. This is not to say since it is true it is not actually a paradox, it very much is. To solve the paradox is to make a heresy. Is anything too great for God?
3. Jesus has a reasonable soul. He was not pure divinity just under his skin, he had a soul just like ours. In John 11, Jesus wept because his SOUL ACHED. His soul was troubled and rejoiced and hurt. He loved his people and hated evil and felt sorrow and pity for the needy and indignation at those using the temple for trading. He was made like his brothers in EVERY respect. He felt temptation just like us.
