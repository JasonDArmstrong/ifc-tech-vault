#### Equipment Notes
1. Mark beginning of sermon by clicking before the first word, pressing CTL+b, typing start, then press enter
2. after end of sermon prayer is finished stop Audacity recording and OBS video recording
3. Trim sermon
4. Press ctl+a to highlight the entire sermon
5. click on effect and select Normalize
6. check Remove DC offset
7. check Normalize peak amplitude to -1.0 (leave Normalize stereo unchecked)
8. click apply
9. sample several places in sermon and verify audio sounds good
10. Click on file and select export audio
11. verify folder, C:\Users\AudioTech\Music\Service_Recordings\2026_Audacity_Export
12. Configure MP3 settings:
	1. Format MP3
	2. Channels Mono
	3. Sample rate: 4800 Hz
	4. Bit Rate Mode: Constant
	5. Quality 128 kbps
13. Click on entire project then export
14. Ctl+s to save edited audacity file then close Audacity
15. Navigate to IFC Dashboard then Upload Audio
16. Click on scan folder (full speed) upload should take about 30 seconds. if the sermon does not reach 100% quickly or gets stuck click cancel, refresh page and start over at scan folder
17. Verify last export is selected and click upload selected.
18. Do not leave page until sermon is finished uploading
19. click back to dashboard
20. click refresh, not full refresh and verify the sermon is published
21. Check for uploaded sermon on sermonaudio.com
