##### Title- Foolish man faithful God 

- Speaker-Bill Boekestein
- Scripture lesson-Genesis 16:1-16
- Psalms and Hymns-117B  86B(1, 2, 5, 7,)  572  502  Romans doxology 

*its easy to say you love Jesus while you are around others who love Jesus...*

has there ever been a time in your life where you wanted something so bad you where ready to do anything to get it? our desires even for good things can lead us down the wrong path.... such as Sarai giving Abram her maidservant to bear his children.  wanting a child is a wonderful desire however instead of trusting Gods promise of children Sarai and Abram put there trust in the world. often when we want something that god does not have willed for you you begin scheming and trying to force his hand. witch John Calvin refers to as FOOLISH COUNCIL!... pursuing a method of obtaining Gods blessing the way Abram and Sarai did is sin... god ordains both the ends and the needs.. will you learn from Abram and Sarai? the sins of our forefathers are told us so we can learn from them.. be aware of the sins of Abram and Sarai's sin for it left a wave of destruction. and you might say that it was Sarai's fault for proposing it but as the leader of his wife Abram he should have taught godliness and led with godliness so that it wouldn't have even been an option in either of there heads. God cares for you even when you make bad decisions and when others make bad decisions that affect you. all in all don't force Gods hand. and trust in his promises
