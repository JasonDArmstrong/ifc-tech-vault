#### Sermon Notes
Lot's wife
Sodom and Gomorrah is total destruction
nothing will grow there to this day
was told not to look back, stop, or turn around
lot's wife lagged behind (well, maybe) and looked back. Jesus in Luke 17 says that she had some regrets.
lot's wife is an illustration that those who long for the world will be swept away in the end
just a simple disobedience would be enough, but it was worse than that.
her life was in Sodom, and she desired to keep it. she therefore lost her life.
our prize is fellowship with God. looking to anything else is worldliness and evil
we can be thankful for things in this world, but those things are meant to help us pursue our high calling
when Jesus tells us to remember lot's wife, he warns against worldliness
the entire family was destroyed by Lot's worldliness passing on to them.
the man who seeks the kingdom of God second is not wise.
the leader of a household is a gatekeeper of Godliness and the world.
