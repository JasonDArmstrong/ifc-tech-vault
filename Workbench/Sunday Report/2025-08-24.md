	- Abraham 13 years after Hagar, 100 years old. Incredulous  
	- Hagar and Ishmael cast out. 
	- v23 The son of the slave was born according to the flesh
	- The son of the free woman was born through promise
- Meaning
	- Allegorically, Not always hidden meaning, Pilgrims Progress
	- Paul says, the 2 women are 2 covenants
	- Sinai related to people born for slavery. You can only have a relationship in law and are doomed to break it
	- Galatians were being tempted by the law
	- 2nd covenant of above, Chris's works not their performative obedience
- Application - 3 Lessons

		- You must bare fruit. Nothing to do to improve your standing with God.

		- Modern Judaizers are in the church today
		- Don't be surprised
	- Be stubborn

- Conclusion - Are you a slave or free, works or promise.
