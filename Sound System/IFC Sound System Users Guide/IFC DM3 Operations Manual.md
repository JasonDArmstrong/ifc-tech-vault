# IFC Media Systems Operations Manual

## Introduction

The Yamaha DM3 Standard is a standalone, USB‑enabled digital mixing console offering comprehensive signal processing—input gain, 4‑band EQ, dual dynamics, delay, effects and flexible bus routing. Its intuitive touchscreen, user‑defined keys and custom fader banks provide rapid access to every processing block and scene recall.

A sound board’s purpose is to combine microphones, instruments and playback sources; apply corrective and creative processing; and route those signals to front‑of‑house, monitors and recorders. This settings guide details each parameter you’ll use to achieve consistent, professional‑grade mixes.


**Summarizing DM3 settings**

To provide a brief description of the DM3's settings, here's a breakdown:

- **Input**: Analog and digital gain controls.
    
- **EQ**: 4-band parametric EQ with HPF.
    
- **Dyn1**: Gate with default threshold and range.
    
- **Dyn2**: Compressor (threshold, ratio, attack, release).
    
- **Delay**: Channel delay settings.
    
- **FX1/FX2**: Effects bus 1 and 2.
    
- **Send to**: Routes mix/send to matrix.
    
- **Insert**: External insert points.
    
- **Assign**: Channels and patches.
    
- **Automixer**: Auto-mixing function.
    
- **Preset**: Channel libraries.
    
- **CH View/USB**: Overview and file operations.
    
- **Headphones**: Monitoring options.
    
- **Setup**: Various submenus for customization.
